# HookEngine

Turn any article, selected text or YouTube video into posts for X, LinkedIn and TikTok.

HookEngine is a Chrome extension. Open it on the page you want to repurpose, pick the platforms, and copy the drafts. Edit them however you like before you post.

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hookengine-%E2%80%94-content-repu/ikalaemjdindfenbbaecekkocbbocjma)

## How it works

1. **Choose a source.** Read the article in your tab, select some text, or pick a public YouTube video.
2. **Pick platforms.** X threads, LinkedIn posts and TikTok hooks, one or more at a time.
3. **Edit and copy.** Every draft is editable. Copy the version you want and post it yourself.

## Free, with no account

- No sign-in and no card.
- 10 credits a day. An article or text draft costs 1 credit, and a YouTube video costs 5.
- The limit resets at midnight UTC. A shared daily cap applies to all users, so it can pause generation on busy days.
- Your drafts are saved in history on this device only.

## Privacy

Text you submit is sent to HookEngine's server and processed by AI providers to write the drafts. HookEngine does not require an account, and it stores your connection as a salted hash for the daily limit only. The full details are in the [privacy policy](https://yazoniplay.github.io/HookEngine/account/privacy.html).
