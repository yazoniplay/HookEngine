NASA explains how Webb can analyze distant planetary atmospheres using infrared observations and transmission spectroscopy.

X THREAD · 5 POSTS
1/ A planet’s atmosphere can leave clues in the light of its star. NASA’s James Webb Space Telescope is built to read those clues in infrared. 2/ Here’s the method: Webb first measures the star’s spectrum, then measures it again when a planet passes in front. 3/ During that transit, some starlight travels through the planet’s atmosphere before reaching the telescope. 4/ Molecules in the atmosphere absorb specific wavelengths. Those missing parts show up as dips in the spectrum. 5/ Researchers compare those dips with known molecular signatures to work out which molecules are present. It’s chemistry, read through starlight.
LINKEDIN POST
A distant planet can leave a chemical fingerprint in starlight. NASA’s James Webb Space Telescope can study an exoplanet’s atmosphere using a technique called transmission spectroscopy. First, Webb observes the star. Then it observes the system again as the planet passes in front of it. Some of the star’s light filters through the planet’s atmosphere on its way to the telescope. Atmospheric molecules absorb particular wavelengths. Those appear as dips in the spectrum. By comparing the dips with reference measurements, researchers can identify molecules in the atmosphere. The useful idea: even when a planet is too far away to study directly, its effect on light can still tell us what it is made of.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Where does my data go?

Text from the page or selected text is sent to HookEngine’s server and processed by AI providers to create drafts. Account and usage data are handled by the service infrastructure. Read the privacy policy. The current policy does not clearly explain how long source text or generated drafts are retained.

Does it work on paywalled or logged-in pages?

HookEngine reads the content visible in your tab; it cannot promise access to content hidden behind a paywall or login. The page text you submit is sent for processing. The privacy policy does not clearly state a retention period for submitted source text.

What happens when I hit the daily limit?

Free includes 5 generations per day. Pro includes 100. When your plan’s allowance is used, the server rejects further generations until the daily allowance resets. You can review your plan in the account portal.

Does it work for YouTube videos without captions?

YouTube video analysis is handled by Gemini, but the current product documentation does not establish whether every video without captions can be processed.

Can I cancel Pro?

Pro billing is connected to PayPal. The current public account page does not clearly document the cancellation steps.

Which browsers?

The extension manifest requires Chrome 116 or later. Other browsers are not currently verified.

Who makes HookEngine?

HookEngine is an independent project. For support, use the email address configured in the page source: support@example.com.
