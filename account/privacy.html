Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to HookEngine. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome Extension and associated services.

2. Data We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password to manage your authentication and usage limits.
  • Usage Data: We track the number of content generations you perform to enforce our free and pro tier limits.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account.
  • Track and limit daily usage according to your plan (Free or Pro).
  • Improve the functionality and performance of HookEngine.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud infrastructure. We implement rigorous security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Services

HookEngine uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • AI Service Providers: We utilize advanced AI models to process your content and generate social media drafts. We do not sell your personal content to third parties.
  • Database and Auth Services: Used for secure user authentication and account management.
  • Payment Processors: Used for secure payment processing. We do not store your credit card information on our own servers.

6. Your Rights

You can request to have your account and data deleted at any time by contacting us via the support channels provided in the extension.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official support channels.
