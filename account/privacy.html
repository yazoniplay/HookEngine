Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Introduction

HookEngine is a free Chrome extension. You do not need an account to use it. This policy explains what information the extension and its service process, and why.

Information we process

To create drafts, the extension sends the following to HookEngine's server:

  • Source content: the article text, selected text or transcript you choose to generate from, the optional title, the page address for YouTube videos, and the platforms you select.
  • Connection data for daily limits: your IP address is combined with a secret salt and converted into a one-way hash. We store only that hash and a count of credits used that day. The raw IP address is never stored or logged. Hashes are removed by a scheduled cleanup after a short retention period (kept for the current UTC day only, then removed).

Information stored on your device

Your draft history is kept in your browser's local storage, the most recent 20 generations. It is not sent to HookEngine. You can clear it at any time from the extension's History section. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • Create the drafts you request.
  • Enforce the daily credit limit per connection and the shared daily cap.
  • Keep the service running and protect it from abuse.

Third-party services

HookEngine uses the following services to operate:

  • AI providers: your source content is processed by AI models to generate drafts. Providers may change over time, and each handles the content under its own terms. We do not sell your content.
  • Hosting and database: Supabase hosts HookEngine's functions and the database that holds the limit counters described above.

The extension does not use advertising or analytics services.

Accounts created before this change

Earlier versions of HookEngine used sign-in accounts. The extension no longer uses them, and any saved sign-in is removed from your browser when the extension runs. If you have an existing account and want its data deleted, contact us through the support address below.

Your choices

You control what you submit, and you can clear your local history at any time. You can request deletion of any server-side data associated with you through the support address below.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our data practices change, and the date above will change with it.
